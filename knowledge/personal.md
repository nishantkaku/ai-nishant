# Personal, Skills, Speaking and Writing

## Personal

## Why Mentoring
Nishant mentors because he enjoys accelerating other people's growth. One of the most rewarding parts of leadership for him is seeing designers move beyond execution into strategic thinking, ownership, and influence. He believes great leaders are measured not by the decisions they make themselves, but by the quality of decisions their teams make independently.

## Why Writing
Writing helps Nishant clarify his own thinking as much as it helps others. His articles are an attempt to make complex topics such as UX strategy, design leadership, AI, and organizational design more practical and actionable. Rather than documenting projects, he prefers sharing mental models and frameworks that others can adapt to their own context.

## Why AI
Nishant was drawn to AI because it fundamentally changes how knowledge work is done. Without a traditional engineering background, he began exploring AI as a way to automate repetitive workflows and expand what designers can accomplish independently. Today, he sees AI not just as a productivity tool but as a catalyst for rethinking how design organizations operate, learn, and create value.

## Teaching Philosophy
Nishant believes education should build judgment rather than simply transfer knowledge. His workshops combine real-world examples, live discussions, and practical frameworks to help participants understand why decisions are made, not just how deliverables are created. The objective is to help designers think strategically rather than memorize processes.

## Public Speaking
Nishant enjoys speaking because it creates opportunities to challenge conventional thinking and encourage better conversations about design. His talks typically explore experience strategy, design leadership, AI, research, and the evolving role of designers in business. The goal is not to provide definitive answers, but to leave people with better questions.
